\section*{Notation}

There are three players. Player \(p\)'s decision vector is
\[
  x_p = (x_{p,1},x_{p,2},x_{p,3},x_{p,4},x_{p,5}) \in \mathbb{R}^5.
\]
The bilevel structure used in the tests is:
\[
  \text{upper level controls coordinates } 4,5,
  \qquad
  \text{lower level controls coordinates } 1,2,3.
\]
For each player problem below, the other players' decisions are treated as
fixed parameters, as in a generalized Nash equilibrium. The three player
problems are solved simultaneously.

\section*{Player 1}

Player 1 solves
\[
\begin{aligned}
  \min_{x_1 \in \mathbb{R}^5}
  \quad &
    (x_{1,4}-1.20)^2 + (x_{1,5}-0.80)^2 \\
  \text{subject to}
  \quad &
    x_1 \in \mathcal{S}_1(x_2,x_3).
\end{aligned}
\]
The lower-level solution set is
\[
\begin{aligned}
  \mathcal{S}_1(x_2,x_3)
  =
  \operatorname*{arg\,min}_{y_1 \in \mathbb{R}^5}
  \quad &
    (y_{1,1}-1.00)^2
    +(y_{1,2}-0.90)^2
    +(y_{1,3}-0.15)^2\\
  \text{subject to}
  \quad &
    0.6375 - y_{1,1} - 0.25x_{2,1} - 0.25x_{3,1} \ge 0,\\
  &
    2.4250 - y_{1,2} - 0.25x_{2,2} - 0.25x_{3,2} \ge 0,\\
  &
    y_{1,3} + 0.25x_{2,3} + 0.25x_{3,3} - 0.8625 \ge 0,\\
  &
    y_{1,4} + 0.25x_{2,4} + 0.25x_{3,4} - 0.8750 \ge 0.
\end{aligned}
\]

  \text{subject to}
  \quad &
    x_2 \in \mathcal{S}_2(x_1,x_3).
\end{aligned}
\]
The lower-level solution set is
\[
\begin{aligned}
  \mathcal{S}_2(x_1,x_3)
  =
  \operatorname*{arg\,min}_{y_2 \in \mathbb{R}^5}
  \quad &
    (y_{2,1}-1.05)^2
    +(y_{2,2}-1.00)^2
    +(y_{2,3}-0.20)^2\\
  \text{subject to}
  \quad &

  &
    y_{2,4} + 0.25x_{1,4} + 0.25x_{3,4} - 0.9500 \ge 0.
\end{aligned}
\]

\section*{Player 3}

Player 3 solves
\[
\begin{aligned}
  \min_{x_3 \in \mathbb{R}^5}
  \quad &
    (x_{3,4}-1.40)^2 + (x_{3,5}-1.00)^2 \\
  \text{subject to}
  \quad &
    x_3 \in \mathcal{S}_3(x_1,x_2).
\end{aligned}
\]
The lower-level solution set is
\[
\begin{aligned}
  \mathcal{S}_3(x_1,x_2)
  =
  \operatorname*{arg\,min}_{y_3 \in \mathbb{R}^5}
  \quad &
    (y_{3,1}-1.10)^2
    +(y_{3,2}-1.10)^2
    +(y_{3,3}-0.25)^2\\
  \text{subject to}
  \quad &
    0.7125 - y_{3,1} - 0.25x_{1,1} - 0.25x_{2,1} \ge 0,\\
  &
    2.5750 - y_{3,2} - 0.25x_{1,2} - 0.25x_{2,2} \ge 0,\\
  &
    y_{3,3} + 0.25x_{1,3} + 0.25x_{2,3} - 0.9375 \ge 0,\\
  &
    y_{3,4} + 0.25x_{1,4} + 0.25x_{2,4} - 1.0250 \ge 0.
\end{aligned}
\]

\section*{Expected Equilibrium}

The expected equilibrium used in the tests is
\[
\begin{aligned}
  x_1^\star &= (0.40,\;0.90,\;0.55,\;1.20,\;0.80),\\
  x_2^\star &= (0.45,\;1.00,\;0.60,\;1.30,\;0.90),\\
  x_3^\star &= (0.50,\;1.10,\;0.65,\;1.40,\;1.00).
\end{aligned}
\]

At this point, each player's first and third constraints are active, and each
player's second and fourth constraints have slack equal to \(1\). For example,
for Player 1,
\[
\begin{aligned}
  0.6375 - 0.40 - 0.25(0.45) - 0.25(0.50) &= 0,\\
  2.4250 - 0.90 - 0.25(1.00) - 0.25(1.10) &= 1,\\
  0.55 + 0.25(0.60) + 0.25(0.65) - 0.8625 &= 0,\\
  1.20 + 0.25(1.30) + 0.25(1.40) - 0.8750 &= 1.
\end{aligned}
\]

\section*{Nonlinear Test Variant}

The nonlinear benchmark in the tests uses the same expanded player problems and
the same expected equilibrium, but replaces every squared-distance term
\[
  (z-a)^2
\]
by
\[
  \cosh(z-a)-1,
\]
and replaces every linear inequality residual \(r(z) \ge 0\) by
\[
  \exp(r(z))-1 \ge 0.
\]
This preserves the same active and inactive constraints.
